# IM&T Brand Identity Manual
**Version:** 1.0  
**Core Concept:** The Engine of Constructed Worlds  
**Status:** Active / Living Document

---

## 1. The Brand Philosophy
IM&T is not just a studio; it is a mechanical process of turning raw chaos (data, AI, imagination) into structured, human-usable reality. We sit at the intersection of **High-Tech Architecture** and **Analog Craft**.

### Brand Pillars
* **Momentum:** We move from idea to artifact with engineering precision.
* **Order from Chaos:** We use technical frameworks to shape nebulous, complex inputs.
* **Tactile Digitalism:** Even our digital outputs should feel "heavy," intentional, and human.
* **The Modular Truth:** Every part of our ecosystem must be able to stand alone.

---

## 2. Visual Identity (The Aesthetic DNA)

### 2.1 Artistic Style: "The Etched Reality"
All visual assets must adhere to the **Woodcut/Linocut** aesthetic. 
* **Texture:** High-grain, distressed, tactile, and organic.
* **Contrast:** Extreme high-contrast (Black & White). No soft gradients; use hatching and cross-hatching for shading.
* **Complexity:** Intricate linework that rewards close inspection.

### 2.2 Logo Composition Rules
* **The Seal:** Primary logos should be contained within a circular, weathered seal.
* **The Breakout:** To represent "momentum" and "breaking boundaries," key elements (tracks, smoke, light beams, tool edges) should intentionally break the circular frame.
* **Symmetry vs. Dynamism:** Use geometric symmetry for the "structure" and organic, swirling lines for the "chaos."

### 2.3 Typography
* **Primary (Headings):** High-contrast, classic Serif (e.g., *Playfair Display, EB Garamond, or Caslon*). This conveys authority and timelessness.
* **Secondary (Body/Technical):** Clean, functional Monospace or geometric Sans-Serif (e.g., *JetBrains Mono or Roboto Mono*). This conveys the "Engineering" aspect.

### 2.4 Color Palette
* **Primary:** `#000000` (True Black) and `#F5F5F5` (Off-White/Paper Texture).
* **Accent (Optional):** Only used for "State" changes (e.g., a single deep amber for a "Warning" or "Active" state in UI), but always rendered with a textured, non-digital feel.

## 3. Tone of Voice (The Verbal Identity)
The IM&T voice is **"The Reliable Architect."** It provides the structural standard for all underlying projects. It is precise, grounded, and focused on the *how*.

* **Do:** Use structural and modular language (*Layer, Interface, Configuration, Environment, Observability, Integrity, Deploy*).
* **Do:** Focus on the "Engineer's Experience"-making the complex feel manageable and testable.
* **Don't:** Use "Magic" or "Black Box" language. Avoid implying the AI is a mystery; treat it as a system to be measured.
* **Don't:** Use "Hype" or "Marketing" fluff.

**Example (Applied to imeng):**
* *Bad:* "imeng makes cool AI tools that are super easy to use!"
* *imeng (under IM&T):* "Architecting modular AI test racks and tools to stress-test and measure model assumptions."

---

## 4. Brand Application Examples
* **Technical Documentation:** Hierarchical, Markdown-first, heavy use of code blocks and structured tables.
* **CLI/Tooling UI:** High-contrast, monospaced, emphasizing clear status indicators and configuration visibility.
* **System Architecture Diagrams:** Using the "Etched" style to represent data flow and layer boundaries.
